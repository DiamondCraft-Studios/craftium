# Landmark room sizes (usual, not rules)

Use this only when the ruleset and the brief give no size. These are starting guesses to adapt, never targets, and
you say when you use or depart from one. They come from general Minecraft building habits and recollection of vanilla
structures, NOT from measured files, so they are unverified: check any figure that matters against the Minecraft Wiki
(minecraft.wiki) before relying on it.

## Why these ranges

- A player is 2 blocks tall and a door is 1 by 2, so a room under 3 blocks high feels like a crawlspace and a
  doorway under 2 high cannot be walked through.
- A room has to hold its contents: a chest is 1 block and a spawner is 1 block, but the player needs space to reach
  them and to fight.

## Usual inside sizes (adapt to the brief)

| Room kind | Inside (w x d x h) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Closet, secret space | 1 to 3 wide, 2 to 3 deep, 3 high | a hiding place |
| Small chamber (guard room, cell) | 5 to 7 x 5 to 7 x 4 | room for one spawner and a chest or two |
| Large hall (burial chamber, temple nave) | 9 to 13 x 9 to 15 x 5 to 7 | space to move and see the contents |
| Boss room | 11 to 17 x 11 to 17 x 6 to 9 | a fight needs room and height |
| Entrance, doorway | 1 to 3 wide x 2 to 3 high | 2 high is the least a player fits through |

## Towers and temples

- A tower storey is usually 4 to 5 blocks high inside, with the floor and ceiling adding 1 each.
- A temple room is often taller than a dungeon room because height reads as importance (6 to 9 blocks).

## Where the figures came from

Marked unverified on purpose: no wiki page was read while writing this. If the user's ruleset gives a size, it
replaces all of the above.
